### Less searching. Faster access to what you use every day.

Ghost is a portable productivity hub for Windows that brings together prompts, Markdown files, websites, procedures, and applications in one place. Organize your content by category, find what you need via global search, and access your tools using the "notch" on the edge of your screen.

**Windows x64 · Portable · Local data · Customizable interface**

## What you can do

- **Save and reuse prompts:** organize text by category and copy it for use in your favorite tools.
- **Organize Markdown files:** keep your library of `.md` files accessible for reuse.
- **Gather websites and procedures:** save recurring URLs and instructions, with links to open sites or copy text.
- **Find content via global search:** locate items without manually browsing through each library.
- **Access via the notch:** open shortcuts and quick lists from the screen edge, with configurable positioning and items.
- **Check copy history:** access captured text, links, and images based on your preferences.
- **Manage applications:** organize programs and use WinGet integration to search for and install packages.
- **Customize the experience:** adjust the theme, accent color, font, and animations, as well as your profile.
- **Preserve your data:** use backup and restore features to transfer or recover your organized content.

## Why Ghost?

Ghost brings the content you reuse closer to the actions you perform in Windows. Whether it's a prompt, a link, a procedure, or an application, everything lives in a personal hub offering quick access and a customizable look.

The portable package includes the .NET runtime. Extract the entire folder and open `ghost.exe` without needing to install the runtime separately. Application data is stored locally in the `save` folder.
